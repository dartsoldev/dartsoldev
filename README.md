## About

DartSol LLC is a software company building AI-powered systems, scalable SaaS products, and automation infrastructure for modern businesses.

We work at the intersection of artificial intelligence and full-stack engineering — helping startups launch faster, businesses automate smarter, and enterprises modernize their core operations.

Our clients include early-stage startups, growth-stage SaaS companies, digital agencies, and enterprise teams that need production-ready software delivered with precision and speed.

**Mission:** To build intelligent software that creates compounding value — systems that don't just function, but grow smarter over time.

**Vision:** A world where every business, regardless of size, can operate with the leverage of enterprise-grade AI infrastructure.

## Project Showcase

<br/>

### AI Resume Builder SaaS
A multi-tenant SaaS platform that generates ATS-optimized resumes using AI. Users input career data, select templates, and receive tailored resumes with real-time AI suggestions.

`Next.js` `OpenAI` `MongoDB` `Stripe` `Tailwind CSS`

> Reduced resume creation time by 85%. Reached 2,000+ users within 60 days of launch.

---

### Smart CRM Automation System
A full-stack CRM with embedded AI automation — lead scoring, follow-up sequencing, and deal pipeline management. Integrated with email, calendar, and third-party sales tools.

`React` `Node.js` `PostgreSQL` `n8n` `OpenAI`

> Automated 70% of manual sales operations for a B2B SaaS client, increasing pipeline throughput by 3x.

---

### AI Customer Support Agent
An intelligent support agent trained on company documentation and product data. Handles tier-1 queries autonomously, escalates edge cases, and logs all interactions to a CRM.

`LangChain` `LangGraph` `FastAPI` `Supabase` `OpenAI`

> Deflected 60% of inbound support tickets for a SaaS company with 10,000+ active users.

---

### E-Commerce Automation Engine
An end-to-end automation layer for e-commerce operations — order routing, inventory sync, supplier notifications, review aggregation, and dynamic pricing workflows.

`n8n` `Node.js` `MongoDB` `Shopify API` `Make`

> Eliminated 40+ hours of manual weekly operations for a mid-market e-commerce brand.

---

### Admin Dashboard with RBAC
A production-grade internal dashboard with role-based access control, real-time analytics, user management, audit logs, and data export capabilities.

`Next.js` `Express` `PostgreSQL` `Docker` `Tailwind CSS`

> Deployed across 3 enterprise clients managing $2M+ in monthly transaction volume.

---

### Intelligent Document Processing System
An AI pipeline that ingests unstructured documents (PDFs, contracts, invoices) and extracts structured data, flags anomalies, and routes outputs to downstream systems.

`FastAPI` `LangChain` `HuggingFace` `AWS S3` `PostgreSQL`

> Reduced document processing time from hours to seconds at a legal tech firm.
